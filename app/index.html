Flood judgements are made by Muse Spark from satellite photos and are not field-verified. In testing on 20 tiles, 1 of 666 truly cut-off buildings was marked reachable; how often an "isolated" call is right depends on how flooded the area is (roughly 40-50% to 98%). Road and building outlines come from SpaceNet 8 (Hansch et al., CVPRW 2022). Basemap: OpenStreetMap contributors.
