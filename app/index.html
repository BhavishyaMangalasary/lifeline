How well does it work?

Of 86 truly reachable buildings in the 15 tiles: 23 cleared, 52 left uncertain, 11 wrongly called isolated.

  • The 15 tiles were picked at random from tiles with at least 5 flooded road pieces, so they are heavily flooded by design.
  • The one miss is on tile 541, a small tile with 2 truly cut-off buildings.
  • Buildings are clustered inside tiles, so the evidence is closer to 20 areas than to 666 independent cases.
  • SpaceNet labels a building as flooded when water is in close proximity. This is a proxy, not a field survey.
  • One region and one event (Hurricane Ida, Louisiana). Not tested elsewhere.

Flood judgements are made by Muse Spark from satellite photos and are not field-verified. In testing on 20 tiles, 1 of 666 truly cut-off buildings was marked reachable; how often an "isolated" call is right depends on how flooded the area is (roughly 40-50% to 98%). Road and building outlines come from SpaceNet 8 (Hansch et al., CVPRW 2022). Basemap: OpenStreetMap contributors.
